# Field Research: Semi-Structured Interview Guide
**Smart India Hackathon 2026 — PS SIH26229**  
**Ministry of Mines & JNARDDC | Kabadiwala Connect**

---

## 1. Research Protocol & Ethical Guardrails

- **Target Participants:** Informal door-to-door waste pickers, street *kabadiwalas*, intermediate aggregators (*thok-vyapari*), and CPCB/SPCB authorized recyclers.
- **Language Mode:** Conducted in participant's mother tongue (मराठी, हिन्दी, ਪੰਜਾਬੀ).
- **Informed Consent:** Verbal informed consent obtained prior to interview. Audio recording only with explicit permission.
- **Data Minimization:** No personal phone numbers, Aadhaar, PAN, or full names stored in field notes. Pseudonyms assigned (e.g. `Collector-DL-01`, `Collector-MH-04`).

---

## 2. Participant Profile 1: Informal Waste Collector (*Kabadiwala*)

### 2.1 Daily Operations & Volume
1. How many hours per day do you collect scrap, and what areas do you cover?
2. What are the top 3 electronic or metal items you collect most frequently?
3. How do you weigh and categorize scrap before selling to an aggregator?
4. Do you use your smartphone for work (WhatsApp, calculator, voice notes, UPI)?

### 2.2 Pricing, Negotiation & Income
1. How do you find out today's market price for copper, brass, printed circuit boards, and batteries?
2. How often do you feel the scrap buyer/aggregator undervalues your materials or tampers with the scale?
3. If an authorized recycler offered +15% to +20% higher rate, what would prevent you from selling directly to them?

### 2.3 Payment Methods & Cash Reality
1. What percentage of your scrap sales are settled in physical cash vs UPI/bank transfer?
2. When buyers give you partial cash advance and keep a pending balance, how do you track what they owe you?
3. What happens if a buyer delays or defaults on paying their pending balance?

### 2.4 Safety & Hazardous E-Waste Handling
1. Have you ever handled swollen lithium-ion phone batteries, CRT monitors, or lead-acid batteries?
2. What precautions or safety gear (gloves, masks, eye protection) do you use when dismantling electronics?
3. Who do you contact if an acid burn or toxic smoke incident occurs?

---

## 3. Participant Profile 2: Middle Aggregator (*Thok Vyapari*)

1. How do you source materials from informal collectors across your cluster?
2. What is your typical storage capacity and turnover cycle for e-waste fractions?
3. Do you currently file Form 6 manifests or maintain EPR compliance records?
4. What is your primary challenge when selling consolidated scrap to large smelters or authorized recyclers?

---

## 4. Participant Profile 3: CPCB-Authorized Recycler / Smelter

1. What percentage of your e-waste intake comes from formal institutions vs informal aggregators?
2. What are your biggest bottlenecks in meeting Ministry of Environment / CPCB Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) targets?
3. How do you verify material purity, weight calibration, and absence of child labor in your upstream supply chain?
4. What would motivate you to adopt a digital escrow and traceability platform for small informal lots?
